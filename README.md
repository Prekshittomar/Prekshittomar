## About Me

I'm a Data and Knowledge Engineering graduate student focused on turning LLMs and agentic systems into things that actually ship — not just notebooks. Over the past two years I've worked across the full AI application lifecycle: prompt engineering and evaluation, RAG pipelines with vector search, multi-agent orchestration (CrewAI, AutoGen), and shipping the resulting systems as usable dashboards and assistants for non-technical stakeholders.

I care most about the gap between "the model works in a demo" and "the model is reliable in production" — context engineering, hallucination reduction, and evaluation benchmarks are where I spend most of my time. Two of my applied research projects have been published in *Empirical Economics Letters*.

Currently exploring knowledge graphs and how they combine with retrieval-augmented generation for more grounded, source-cited AI systems.
